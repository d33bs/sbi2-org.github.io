Premier European Meeting of the Society of Biomolecular Imaging and Informatics (SBI2)

Thank you to everyone who joined us in Cambridge for the first-ever European meeting of the Society of Biomolecular Imaging and Informatics (SBI2). Your energy and engagement made our inaugural gathering such a success – we couldn’t have done it without you.

 Over two days, we came together as a community united by a shared passion for imaging and informatics. The programme was filled with insight, collaboration, and lively discussion – from inspiring keynotes and thought-provoking poster sessions to valuable networking moments. A special thank you to our speakers, sponsors, and vendors, whose contributions made this first edition possible.

About Us

The Society of Biomolecular Imaging and Informatics (SBI2) is an international community of leaders, scientists, and students promoting technological advancement, discovery, and education to quantitatively interrogate biological models to provide high context information at the cellular level.
